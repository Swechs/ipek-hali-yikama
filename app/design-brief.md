# Design Brief — İpek Halı Yıkama

## Design read
A local carpet cleaning business in Isparta, Turkey — warm, trustworthy, and service-oriented. The emotional register is professional yet approachable, speaking directly to homeowners who want clean carpets without hassle.

## Concept spine
The site is a **transparent service counter** — prices, process, and contact are immediately visible, like walking into a clean, well-organized local shop where everything is labeled and the staff greets you at the door.

## Delivery tier
`editorial` — calm, minimal, typography-driven with micro-motion only. A local service business needs speed, clarity, and trust — not cinematic scrolling.

## Animation mode: non-animated — user's business is a local service site; speed and clarity over animation

## Locked palette
- `#fdfbf7` cream-white (background)
- `#f9f5ed` warm cream (sections)
- `#2d6046` deep green (primary brand)
- `#3a7d5b` medium green (accents)
- `#e8f5ee` light green (badges/tags)
- `#3d2e1e` warm dark (text)
- `#7a6a5a` warm mid (secondary text)
- `#25D366` WhatsApp green (CTAs)

Defense: Cream/green is warm, clean, and nature-adjacent — appropriate for a cleaning service that wants to convey hygiene, trust, and local warmth. Avoids the banned graphite+orange, black+neon, and beige+oxblood families.

## Locked type
Inter (400/500/600/700/800) — clean geometric sans-serif, excellent Turkish character support, no serif needed for a service business.

## Section plan
1. **Hero** — value proposition + price highlights + dual CTA (phone/WhatsApp)
2. **Services grid** — 3 service cards (halı/koltuk/perde)
3. **Process steps** — 4-step numbered flow
4. **Service areas** — tag cloud of locations
5. **FAQ preview** — expandable Q&A
6. **CTA banner** — dark green strip with phone/WhatsApp

## CTA inventory
- **Hero phone CTA** — solid green pill with phone icon
- **Hero WhatsApp CTA** — WhatsApp green pill
- **Hero calculator CTA** — outline green pill
- **Floating phone FAB** — circular green, bottom-right
- **Floating WhatsApp FAB** — circular #25D366, bottom-right
- **Banner CTA** — inverse white pill on dark green

## Pages
1. `/` — Home (hero + services + process + areas + FAQ + CTA)
2. `/hali-yikama` — Carpet cleaning with interactive calculator
3. `/koltuk-yikama` — Upholstery cleaning
4. `/perde-yikama` — Curtain cleaning (no price — directs to contact)
5. `/hizmet-bolgeleri` — Service areas with Atabey/Eğirdir schedules
6. `/iletisim` — Contact page
7. `/sss` — Full FAQ with 12 questions
